PixRefine Support

Import or save a photo

Use the library’s import menu to choose a photo from Photos or Files. To save an edited copy, open the editor’s export menu and choose an available format or Save Copy to Photos.

Restore or manage PixRefine Pro

In the PixRefine library, open the More menu, choose PixRefine Pro, then select Restore Purchases. Use the Apple Account you originally subscribed with. To check renewal or cancel, open Apple Subscriptions.
