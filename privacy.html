PixRefine Privacy Policy

Photos and projects

PixRefine processes photos and edits on your device. Photos you import and projects you create are stored in the app’s local project library. PixRefine accesses your photo library when you use features that read or save photos. You can manage photo access in iOS Settings.

Exports and sharing

When you export or share a photo, the destination you choose receives a copy. Depending on your export settings, that copy may include photo metadata such as location. You can remove projects from PixRefine’s library. Copies saved or shared elsewhere can be removed from their respective destinations.

Subscriptions

Apple processes subscription purchases and payments. PixRefine uses StoreKit to check whether your subscription is active.
